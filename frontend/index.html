Our Story

Handcrafted in Mathara, baked for every celebration

Biscotti Cake Factory bakes seven distinct collections — from everyday classics to opulent royal creations — each one made to order with real cream, quality couverture and honest ingredients.

Every cake on our menu is built around one idea: familiar flavours, elevated by careful craft. Whether it's a simple birthday or a grand celebration, there's a Biscotti cake for the moment — and if you don't see it on the menu, our Bake Room team will build it from scratch.
